agent-guard and similar systems: Giovanni does not want approval prompts or queues ('not a babysitter for the AI'). Safety comes from structure (closed tool lists, protected paths, validated/formatted data between agents, the taskmaster deciding from what it sees). Actuators may run anything except sudo. The system must stay functional, fast and small (bounded logs); a full disk caused by the system is a system failure.
