Animation guideline part 2 of 2 (part 1: design.animation-principles). (7) Solidity: nothing teleports or pops; things enter, leave and transform continuously. (8) Exaggerate for readability, never against the facts: enlarging or slowing is labelled (not to scale), what happens is never changed. (9) Readable in greyscale; project palette, one meaning per colour; text contrast at least 4.5:1; clear silhouettes; pixel art at whole-number scale with clean one-pixel lines. (10) No decorative motion that teaches nothing. (11) prefers-reduced-motion turns movement into fades or stepped stills that keep the meaning; never flash more than 3 times per second; stop animating off-screen or in a hidden tab. (12) Proof: watch it in a real browser at normal speed and frame by frame, plus reduced motion and phone width, steady ~50+ fps; report what was observed. Sources: Thomas and Johnston 1981; Lasseter 1987; Chang and Ungar 1993; Tversky, Morrison and Betrancourt 2002; Mayer multimedia principles; NN/g animation duration; Material motion; WCAG 2.2 SC 2.2.2, 2.3.1, 2.3.3; Arnheim, Gestalt, Albers, Tufte.
