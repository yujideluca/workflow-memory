Animation guideline part 1 of 2 (Giovanni 2026-11-19; part 2: design.animation-craft). Every animation built for Giovanni (UI motion, teaching scenes, pixel art) follows it. (1) Purpose: animate only when change over time is the point; else a still diagram. (2) One idea per moment: one focal point, the rest dimmed or still. (3) Viewer control: teaching scenes get play/pause, step back/forward and a time or speed slider; start paused or stop after one loop; nothing moves on its own over 5 s without a pause control. (4) Name the parts before they move; labels next to the object; captions when the thing happens. (5) Natural motion: easing on everything (linear only for mechanical or constant processes), arcs for organic paths, follow-through and overlap, anticipation before big moves, squash and stretch only where matter really deforms, volume kept. (6) Timing: UI transitions 150-300 ms, max 400-500 ms for big changes, exits ~20% faster than entrances; teaching scenes slow enough to follow on first view, with holds on key poses.
