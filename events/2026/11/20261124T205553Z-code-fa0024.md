Ralexandria pictures must never look smeared (Giovanni 2026-11-24, on an atlas drawing): erasing a printed label never blends neighbouring pixels; a label on plain ground takes that tone, a label on hatching stays as printed (atlas) or loses only its letter strokes (hand-made figures). Requirement RX-42.
