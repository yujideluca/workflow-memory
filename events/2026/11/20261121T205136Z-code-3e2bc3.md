Ralexandria (Giovanni 2026-11-21): every anatomy entry needs a labelled schematic drawn from an openly licensed original (Gray's 1918 public domain, Servier CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia CC BY/BY-SA; pixelized, numbered, legend as page text, credit in caption) and a structure map (tree of parts/segments/branches plus the course where there is one). Anatomy cannot be learnt without them.
