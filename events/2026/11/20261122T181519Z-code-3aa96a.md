A background subagent that has not reported after about 15 minutes is checked by looking, not by waiting: are its files still changing, is any process of it running. If neither, take over its state (run the gate, verify, continue). Briefs for subagents forbid background commands and require stdin and a timeout on every program run. Learned 2026-11-22: a subagent finished its code in 15 minutes, then hung, and nobody looked for almost 3 hours.
