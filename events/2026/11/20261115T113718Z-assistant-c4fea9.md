Giovanni 2026-11-15: when checking facts against the literature, never settle for abstracts: they say almost nothing. Read the full article. Thousands of articles are open on the internet, and authors often post the full text themselves (e.g. on ResearchGate, institutional repositories, preprint servers); look for those copies. A claim supported only by an abstract counts as not verified.
