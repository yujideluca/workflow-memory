Giovanni 2026-11-15 (correcting Claude, who had proposed merging them into one finding): variant entries of a finding in ultrasound, CT and MRI (e.g. focal / diffuse bladder wall thickening) are NOT merged. They are grouped as variants of the characterisation of the same finding inside the same imaging method. Each imaging method has its own nomenclature and is evaluated separately: nothing is merged, mapped or pooled across methods.
