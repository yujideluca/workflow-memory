Radiology matcher (2026-10-29): the engine and the statistical analysis must use multithreading and the GPU for calculation when the hardware supports it, with a CPU fallback giving the same results. Recorded in ADR-0002 amendment, CLAUDE.md principle 9 and docs/modelling-directives.md section 3b; requirement EN-2 is added with its test in the first engine commit.
